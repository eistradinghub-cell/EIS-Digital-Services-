A few useful answers

Before you get started

Some quick information to help you use the platform with confidence.

Do I need an account to use the platform?

You can look through this page without signing in. To submit and manage requests, create an account and use your dashboard.

Where can I check an application after submitting it?

Sign in and open your dashboard. Application information and any available updates are shown there.

What should I do if I need help?

Sign in to your account and open the Customer Care area to send a message about your question or request.

Can I review service information before applying?

Yes. Read the service description and any displayed requirements before submitting your information.

How do I find items listed for sale?

Open the Marketplace from the website navigation to view the items currently listed and their displayed details.

Please use the platform only for the services and purposes described on the relevant service page. Requirements, processing steps and availability may vary. Do not share passwords, one-time codes or sensitive account credentials with anyone.
